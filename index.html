To create a website that looks professional and "hand-coded" rather than AI-generated, I have avoided generic frameworks like Bootstrap or Tailwind. Instead, I used **Modern CSS (Grid and Flexbox)**, custom typography pairings, and a **"Dark-Mode Gourmand"** aesthetic common in high-end restaurant branding. The design focuses on high-quality spacing (whitespace), subtle micro-interactions, and a mobile-first approach. ### 1. The HTML (index.html) ```html Two Brothers Shawarma | Wardha's OG Shawarma Spot
